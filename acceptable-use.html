Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

Venom must be used lawfully and responsibly.

1. Malware

You may not use Venom to create, distribute, execute, or facilitate malware, spyware, ransomware, credential stealers, destructive payloads, or other malicious software.

2. Credential theft

You may not steal or attempt to obtain passwords, authentication tokens, cookies, payment details, recovery information, or other credentials without authorization.

3. Unauthorized access

You may not use Venom to access accounts, computers, networks, databases, or services without permission.

4. Fraud

Venom may not be used for scams, fraudulent payments, impersonation, account theft, or deceptive activity intended to obtain money, property, credentials, or unauthorized access.

5. Personal information

Venom may not be used for unauthorized surveillance, credential harvesting, doxxing, or unlawful collection or disclosure of personal information.

6. Harassment

You may not use Venom to threaten, stalk, intimidate, or deliberately harass another person.

7. Disruption

You may not intentionally damage, overload, disable, or disrupt computers, networks, websites, services, accounts, or infrastructure.

8. Venom infrastructure

You may not attempt to compromise Venom's website, API, database, authentication service, licensing service, or security controls.

9. Licensing abuse

You may not steal, forge, bypass, reproduce, or unlawfully redistribute licences, authentication tokens, account credentials, or protected access.

10. Enforcement

Venom may restrict or terminate access where an account creates a security risk, abuses the Service, or violates this policy.
