Cookie Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Venom uses cookies and browser storage for authentication, security, and preferences.

1. Cookies currently used

venom_auth
Secure authentication cookie used to maintain a signed-in Venom session.
venom_cookie_choice
Stores your cookie preference so the cookie notice does not appear on every page load.

2. Essential only

Essential authentication and security cookies may still be used when required to provide account functionality you request.

3. Allow cookies

Choosing Allow cookies may permit Venom to keep an authentication session valid for up to 30 days unless revoked or expired sooner.

4. Advertising

Venom does not currently use advertising cookies or third-party behavioural advertising trackers.

5. Other browser storage

Venom may use localStorage or sessionStorage for notification state, temporary security session data, and similar browser-side functionality.

6. Managing cookies

You can remove or block cookies in your browser. Blocking authentication cookies may prevent account features from working correctly.
