Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Venom processes account, authentication, device, and technical information.

1. Account identifiers

Email addresses and usernames submitted during account registration are normalized before being converted into keyed cryptographic lookup values for database matching.

Venom does not need to store plaintext email or username lookup values in the account database.

2. Display usernames

A display username may be stored in encrypted form so Venom can display the username after authentication.

Unlike one-way lookup values, encrypted display data can be decrypted by Venom when the required encryption key is available.

3. Password protection

Passwords are not stored in plaintext and are not stored using reversible encryption.

Password processing uses a server-side secret together with a password derivation function and a unique salt.

4. Technical and network information

When you access Venom, infrastructure may process technical information such as IP address, browser information, language, country or network location, and request metadata.

5. Human verification

Venom may process browser interaction signals and temporary challenge information to help distinguish normal human activity from automated abuse.

6. Authentication sessions

Authentication sessions use randomly generated tokens. Venom stores a cryptographic hash of the authentication token rather than the original token value.

7. Device identifiers

Device information or hardware-related identifiers may be processed for authentication, licensing, fraud prevention, and abuse prevention.

Where practicable, original identifiers may be transformed before persistence.

8. Data minimisation

Venom is designed to limit stored account information to information needed for authentication, security, account functionality, legal records, and abuse prevention.

9. Requests for information

Venom can only provide information it actually possesses or can lawfully access.

Cryptographic protections do not remove legal duties that may apply to information Venom is able to access.

10. Security limitations

Venom uses security controls intended to reduce risk, but no software, network, database, or cryptographic system can guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Venom's systems, products, or legal requirements change.
